Meet the founder · Meridian, Idaho

Jeff Jones

AI Showrunner. Film Director. Your creative partner from the first story conversation to the final cut.

You’ve spent years building a world readers care about. The next step deserves more than a montage of impressive images. It needs a screenplay with a dramatic spine, characters whose choices make sense, and a director who protects the emotional reason your audience turns the page.

I founded Storyforge Imaginarium to bring that work together: writing, character development, visual storytelling, AI production, editing, music, and sound. My role is to guide the show and deliver a coherent creative result.

Your book already has a world. Let’s find its screen language.

For authors, publishers, and authorized rights holders, I develop adaptations through collaboration. We start with what must survive the move from page to screen: the central conflict, the emotional promise, the relationships, and the moments readers would miss if they disappeared.

Then we work in visible stages. You review the direction before it becomes a full production commitment. We refine the treatment, script, characters, and selected scenes together, using each review to make the next decision clearer.

  1. Find the story: identify the dramatic spine and choose a feature, short, or episodic format.
  2. Build the script: shape scenes, dialogue, pacing, reveals, and emotional turns for the screen.
  3. Develop the characters: document motivations, contradictions, relationships, voices, and visual references in character bibles.
  4. Establish the world: build a universe bible, continuity rules, and season architecture where the project needs them.
  5. Make the story visible: direct approved scenes, concept trailers, and pitch assets through an iterative production and editorial process.

Ambition without the logistics of a conventional shoot

Fully generated sequences can be developed without booking physical locations, transporting a camera and lighting crew, or coordinating an on-set cast. That opens room to explore worlds and revise visual ideas that would otherwise require a new shoot.

The craft remains hands-on: directing performance, controlling continuity, selecting takes, shaping the edit, and finishing sound. We define the scope around the work your story needs, with clear fees and review milestones. Rights, approved likenesses, and delivery requirements remain part of production planning.
